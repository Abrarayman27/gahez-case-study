The challenge was everything around the decision to go

Event experiences are fragmented.

Users discover events, compare options, coordinate with friends, choose tickets, manage bookings, and prepare across disconnected moments. The problem isn't ticket purchasing. It's the effort required to turn “I want to go out” into “I'm ready to go.”

How might we make deciding what to do feel effortless?
How might we help friends agree on one plan?
How might we keep people prepared, and informed when plans change?

Understanding the problem before designing the solution

I researched the complete event journey, not just booking: how people discover things to do, what influences decisions, how group preferences shape plans, and how they react when plans change.
